Simulation

Ideal MHD (ARMS) on an adaptively refined grid

No gravity, conduction, or radiation, so no synthetic emission

34 G parasitic polarity in a 2 G open field

Uniform 1.2 MK corona, β ≈ 0.2

cs ≈ 130 km/s, vA ≈ 300 km/s
